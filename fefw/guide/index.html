Guide

How to use this app mid-playthrough, and what the numbers mean.

Set up your army in two minutes

  1. Open My Army and pick your route. Tap Add starting units to bring in your lord and the route's main characters.
  2. Tap + Add units and tap the portrait of everyone else you've recruited. Recruits are listed in the Renown order they unlock on your route.
  3. Open each unit and set Level now and Class now to match the game. That alone shows their growth rates and average gains.
  4. Optional: tick Enter stats from the status screen and copy the nine numbers from the game. You then get projected stats at whatever level you look ahead to.
  5. Optional: add class changes under Class plan ("Brigand until Lv 35, then Warrior"). Every view updates as you change it.

Your army is saved in this browser. Copy army link gives you a link that loads the same army on another device, or lets you share it.

What each page is for

My ArmyYour actual units: where each one is now and where their stats are headed.
PlannerExperiment with one unit's class path. Auto-build picks the best class for each tier for the stats you care about. Units in My Army have an Open in Planner button.
Class FinderEvery class ranked for one character by the stats you pick.
CompareUp to four characters in the same class, side by side, to decide who gets a slot or a class change.
RecruitmentWho you can recruit on each route, at what Renown and support level, with a checklist. Recruited units can be sent to My Army in one tap.
Data menuReference tables (characters, classes, mounts) and Quick Calc for a single character and class.

What the numbers mean

Stats
HP, Str (Strength), Mag (Magic), Spd (Speed), Dex (Dexterity), Def (Defense), Res (Resistance), Lck (Luck) and Cha (Charm): the game's Basic Stats, in its order. Build (Bld) offsets equipment weight and has no growth rate, so it isn't shown.
Growth rate
The chance a stat goes up by 1 on a level-up. A unit's growth = their personal growth + their class's modifier + their mount's bonus. 45% Str means Str rises on roughly 45 of every 100 level-ups.
Class modifier
What a class adds to (or takes from) a unit's growth rates while they're in it.
Class change
Units change class by passing a certification exam (license, Renown and skill ranks such as Sword C or Riding D). Each tier has an ideal level: Beginner 5, Specialty 20, Advanced 35, Master 45. Below it the exam is harder, not impossible.
Average gain
Stat points you can expect over a stretch of levels: growth rate × level-ups. Real results vary; luck evens out over many levels.
Projected stats
The stats you entered, plus average gains, adjusted for the flat stat change each class gives. Only shown for units whose stats you've entered.
Mount bonus
Animals from the Stable raise growth rates while the unit is in a class that can ride them. This app uses the bonus at Bond Level 5 (the max); the level-by-level split isn't known yet.

Colour key

Growth rates and class modifiers use different scales, because +10 on a class is a strong boost while a 10% growth rate is poor. Every coloured table shows its key underneath.

Limits worth knowing

  • Stat caps and base stats aren't published for Fortune's Weave yet, so neither is applied.
  • Some numbers are still unverified (mount bonuses, Charioteer effects); the app says so wherever it uses them. Where Serenes Forest and Game8 disagree, Serenes Forest is used.
  • The Spoilers switch (top right) is off by default: Divine classes, Part III mounts and late-game units stay hidden until you turn it on.

Recaptr

Footy or it didn't happen.

A capture app for people who actually edit what they record. Plug in a capture card, pick a screen or a window, and hit ⌘R. Drop markers from anywhere with ⌃⌥⌘B, even mid-game. When you stop, Recaptr writes a Final Cut file next to the recording, so your clip opens with every marker already in place.

What it does

Capture. Capture cards, cameras, displays, and single windows. Up to 4K at 60 fps, HEVC or H.264.

Mark. Markers go into a Final Cut file next to the recording. With Apple Intelligence on, Recaptr names markers and episodes for you, on your Mac.

File. Set a series and each take saves as the next episode in its own folder.

Survive long sessions. Crash-safe files, separate game and mic tracks, audio that stays in sync over hours, and Instant Replay with ⇧⌘R.

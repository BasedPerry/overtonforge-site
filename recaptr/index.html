Footy or it didn't happen.

A capture app for people who actually edit what they record. Plug in a capture card, pick a screen or a window, and hit ⌘R. Drop markers from anywhere with ⌃⌥⌘B, even mid-game. When you stop, Recaptr writes a Final Cut file next to the recording, so your clip opens with every marker already in place.

// How it works

Record. Mark. Cut.

  1. 01

    Record

    Capture cards, cameras, displays, and single windows. Up to 4K at 60 fps, HEVC or H.264.

    ⌘R

  2. 02

    Mark

    Hit a key when something happens. It works from any app, so you never leave the game.

    ⌘B ⌃⌥⌘B

  3. 03

    Cut

    Every take with markers gets a matching .fcpxml. Double-click it and Final Cut opens the clip with markers in place.

    Series – Ep 4.fcpxml

// What it does

Built for the long session.

Instant Replay

Missed it? Save the last 15 seconds of a screen or window with ⇧⌘R.

Separate tracks

Game and mic land on their own tracks, and audio stays in sync over hours.

Crash-safe files

If anything quits mid-take, the .mov opens in QuickTime with everything up to a few seconds before.

Series and episodes

Set a series and each take saves as the next episode in its own folder.

Names itself

With Apple Intelligence on, Recaptr names markers and episodes for you, on your Mac.

Monitor on the fly

Hear what you're recording, and flip monitoring off with ⌘K.
